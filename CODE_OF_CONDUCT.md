# Community conduct

Grantline welcomes respectful participation regardless of background, experience
or identity. Discuss technical ideas with evidence, assume misunderstandings can
be resolved, accept constructive feedback and give appropriate credit.

Harassment, threats, sexual attention, discriminatory language, disclosure of
private information, impersonation and repeated disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in project issues, discussions, pull requests or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content and restrict participation. Responses
should consider severity, context and repeated behavior, and avoid disclosing
private information. Maintainers are subject to these expectations as well.

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, contact a repository maintainer through
the relevant project discussion without repeating private or harmful details.
For sensitive abuse hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's private **Report abuse** route.
GitHub handles that report; it is not a promise that maintainers receive its contents.
Do not post private evidence publicly. No separate private conduct mailbox is
currently designated. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
